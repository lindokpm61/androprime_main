## Why the cost search came back empty

The testosterone/cost head terms (`testosterone test cost`, `how much does a testosterone test cost`, `testosterone test price`, `cheapest testosterone test`, `male hormone test cost`) all return **no UK volume** in DFS, the same pattern the keyword-backfill found for the category heads: *"demand is expressed through symptoms/conditions, not the category name."* The frustration is real and documented (mainstream-buyer research, VOC), but it searches as three other things. Cost was the wrong axis.

## Shelf 3: The doubt queries (GEO, not ranking)

*"normal testosterone but symptoms," "do I need TRT," "GP won't test me," "NHS won't treat low T"* → **near-zero or very high-KD on Google** (`is my testosterone low` KD 57, `do i have low testosterone` KD 62; the rest null). This sentiment lives in **Reddit, Quora and AI answers**, exactly as `geo-serp-findings` concluded: AI Overviews on 31/35 priority SERPs, **Andro Prime cited 0 times across 48 LLM answers.**

- **Fit:** Pillar F (patient-owned data / GEO flagship, no volume gate).
- **Format:** the "no reason to sell you testosterone" **manifesto** (AI-citation asset) + question-headed H2s and FAQ schema folded into the existing symptom articles (`why-am-i-always-tired`, `myth-of-normal-range`) + Reddit/Trustpilot presence.
- **Verdict:** run as the GEO spoke alongside Shelf 2. Measured by citations, not rank.
