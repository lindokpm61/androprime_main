## Script

**Funnel:** MOFU (Capture) | job: solution-aware correction (testosterone sampling) | cta: quiz | format: short-video | marker: testosterone

**Emotion:** Surprise / curiosity.

**Shock-facts used (all true, all inherited from the hub):**
- Testosterone runs on a daily rhythm, highest in the early morning and drifting down through the day.
- UK guidance is to take the sample between 7 and 11am.
- A sample drawn at 4pm can read low for no reason other than the clock.
- The guidance also says to confirm a low reading on a second morning test rather than act on one.
- Men get told they are low on the strength of an afternoon appointment.

**Addiction loop.** Stakes: he has a result in a drawer or an appointment in the diary, and what he does next depends on whether that number counts. Big question: does his own result actually count. Head fake: the viewer predicts this is going to be "the range is wrong" or "your lab got it wrong", which is where every video about a disappointing result goes; the reveal is that nothing is wrong with the test at all, the swing is a property of the hormone, and one reading was never meant to be acted on by itself. Rehook: "and the guidance does not stop at the morning rule."

**Structure:** C, Breakdown. Chosen over D, Listicle, on avatar fit: a list of sampling rules is more documentation for a man who already owns too much of it (`avatar-mark.md` §A), whereas a breakdown ends with one question he can answer tonight off a sheet he already has.

## Rails held

Written as an allowlist of what the copy does, never a list of what it avoids.

The script stays on the education side of the line throughout. It restates published UK sampling guidance that the Ewa-approved hub already carries and cites, describes a daily rhythm as a normal property of the hormone, and says outright that the test itself is working correctly. Its corrective energy is pointed at the time of an appointment, never at a clinician, a laboratory or the profession. It names no ingredient and no product, so the EFSA tables are not in play. It carries no personal bloodwork figure and no threshold, so there is nothing to substantiate against a filed result and no interpretation of anyone's number is offered. The founder is disclosed at the turn. The marker is testosterone, which Kit 1 measures and which is live, and the framing stays on whether a testosterone reading is valid rather than on what any symptom means, which keeps it clear of the Kit 1 scoping rule entirely. The CTA routes to the test selector.
