## Script

**Funnel:** TOFU (Attract) | job: problem-aware scroll-stop (andropause) | cta: quiz | format: short-video | marker: testosterone
**Emotion:** Recognition / relief.

**Shock-facts used (all true, all inherited from the hub):**
- Nothing on the andropause symptom list is dramatic enough to book an appointment about, and all of it is enough to change your week. That combination is why men wait.
- The same handful of symptoms has several possible causes, and a symptom list cannot separate them.
- The label men reach for names the feeling and not the cause.
- What your blood shows is the only part of this you can measure. Your age is not.

**Addiction loop.** Stakes: two years of feeling flat, nothing obviously wrong, and no single week where it is bad enough to act. Big question: if nothing is dramatic, when do you actually do something about it. Head fake: the viewer predicts this is building to a symptom list, because every andropause video is; the reveal is that the list is the thing keeping him where he is, because it can name the feeling and never the cause. Rehook: "so you give it a name instead."

**Structure:** C, Breakdown. Chosen over A, Personal story, on avatar fit: A would have made the whole piece Keith's biography, and the useful subject here is the viewer's own two years, not Keith's. Keith enters once, at the turn, as someone who waited the same way.

```
[0-5s]   This is what two years felt like. The car still goes. You just
         work harder for the same speed.
         [Visual: his hand pulling the handbrake halfway up and holding
         it there. His own car, real, one prop, no cutaways.]
         [Text: Handbrake half on]

[5-16s]  Energy gone by the afternoon. Sleep that does not put you back
         together. A session that used to cost you one day of soreness
         now costs three. Drive that is quieter than it was.
         [Text: Nothing dramatic]

[16-26s] Here is the part that makes men wait.
         [Pause]
         Nothing on that list is dramatic enough to book an appointment
         about. And all of it is enough to change your week.

[26-40s] So you give it a name instead. Getting older. Stress. The male
         menopause.
         [Slower]
         And a name is not a cause. Those same symptoms can be
         age-related testosterone change, or they can be sleep, stress,
         or lifestyle. A list cannot tell you which one you are.

[turn]   I waited about two years, and I got told I was normal for most
         of them. I started a blood testing company because you cannot
         see a handbrake from outside the car, and what your blood shows
         is the only part of this you can actually measure.

[CTA]    What have you been calling getting older?
         [Text: Education, not medical advice]
```

*Four-check: interesting to Mark, because it names the exact reason he has not acted rather than telling him to act. Compressed, roughly 48 seconds, nothing unload-bearing. The hook stands alone muted: a hand on a half-pulled handbrake with three words over it is a complete idea. End emotion is recognition landing on relief, which is the one aimed at. Compliance, stated as what the copy does: it describes a set of symptoms and names several possible causes for them without attributing them to any one; it says outright that a list cannot separate the causes; it uses the popular label as the thing men reach for rather than as a condition; it carries no figure of any kind; it reports Keith waiting and being told he was normal, which is his own approved bio wording; it discloses the founder at the turn; it routes to the selector.*
