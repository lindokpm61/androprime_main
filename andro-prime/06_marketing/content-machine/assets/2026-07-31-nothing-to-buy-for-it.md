> If you are in your forties or fifties and you have been reading about andropause, here is the part that nobody selling you something will lead with. There is nothing to buy for it.
>
> Andropause, often called the male menopause, is a popular name for the cluster of symptoms some men notice as testosterone gradually declines with age. The decline is real. But the NHS points out the term itself can be misleading, because unlike the menopause there is no single switch-off moment, and a lot of what gets blamed on it has other causes.
>
> That is why the first honest move is not a product. It is finding out where your testosterone actually sits, because a good share of the men reading about this do not have a testosterone problem at all, and money aimed at the wrong cause is money and hope down the drain.
>
> The second move is the unglamorous one, and it does real work here. Losing excess weight, lifting weights, sleeping properly, and easing off alcohol all move how men in this bracket feel. None of it is instant and none of it is glamorous, which is exactly why it gets skipped for something with a label on it.
>
> And some of this does not belong in a wellness test at all. It belongs with your doctor first.
>
> Erectile problems go to your GP rather than to a hormone panel. Erectile difficulty can be an early warning sign of narrowed arteries, because the blood vessels involved are small and show strain first, so it can flag heart risk well before anything else does (British Heart Foundation). If your mood is low, or you have lost interest in things you used to enjoy, please talk to your GP. That is a mental health conversation and it deserves one. Same if this is getting in the way of your daily life or your relationship, and same if you are under 40 with these symptoms, because age-related decline is a less likely explanation that early.
>
> What we do is narrow. We test, we explain the number, and we tell you the next step, including when that next step is your GP. We do not diagnose and we do not prescribe. And if a total testosterone comes back below 12 nmol/L, that is a reason to see your GP, not a reason to buy anything from us.
>
> The full version, including what else these symptoms usually turn out to be and why the time of day your blood is taken matters, is here: andro-prime.com/blog/andropause-male-menopause?utm_source=facebook

- **Nothing in the machine is close to this.** No existing asset names the GP-referral triggers, and none of them says out loud that a result below a threshold is a reason not to buy from us. `the-stack` is the nearest relative (a founder saying do not test yet), but that is a video, its subject is the lifestyle stack, and its de-sell is about timing rather than about which symptoms belong with a doctor.
