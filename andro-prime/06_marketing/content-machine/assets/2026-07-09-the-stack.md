## Script (v2, rewritten to voice 1.2 on 2026-07-31)

**Emotion:** Recognition / relief.

**Addiction loop.** Stakes: Mark is flat by two every afternoon, nothing is obviously wrong, and he has a fortnight before he decides to spend money on something. Big question: if nothing has changed, why does he feel worse. Head fake: a blood testing company is about to tell him to buy a blood test, and instead tells him not to yet. Unguessable in advance, obvious in hindsight, and it is the conflict-free positioning said out loud. Rehook: "and if it does move, you have just saved yourself the price of a test."

**Structure:** B, Problem-solver. Devices 6 (banal scene) and 3 (physical object for an invisible state) stacked.

```
[0-4s]   I run a blood testing company, and I am going to tell you not
         to test yet.
         [Visual: five coasters on a kitchen counter, stacked one at a
         time as he speaks. One prop, one take, no cutaways.]
         [Text: Don't test yet]

[4-14s]  A mate said he was tired all the time. Not sleepy. Flat.
         I asked what had changed. Nothing, he said. Same job, same
         training, same sleep, roughly.
         [Slower]
         That "roughly" is where these things live.

[14-28s] Broken sleep. Stress that never switches off. Training that has
         outrun recovery. A drink most evenings. A breakfast that drops
         you at two.
         [each named as one more coaster goes on the stack]
         He did not have one big problem. He had a stack, and no way to
         see it.

[28-40s] So change two of them. Two, not five, for a fortnight.
         [knocks two off the top]
         [Pause]
         If your energy moves, there it is. You just found it for free.

[turn]   And if it does not move after a fortnight of doing it properly,
         that is when a test earns its place. Not before.
         I would rather tell you that than sell you something you did
         not need yet.

[CTA]    Which two have you been calling roughly the same?
         [Text: Education, not medical advice]
```
