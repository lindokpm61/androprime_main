## Audit: what was taken, and what was missed

### `hook-playbook.md`

Claims to be "synthesised from three studied hook breakdowns (desire-based hooking; the four hook mistakes; the six hook archetypes)".

- **Six archetypes** (Investigator, Contrarian, Experimenter, Teacher, Magician, Fortuneteller): taken verbatim from the B2B framework PDF and re-exampled for ferritin. Faithful.
- **Desire-based hooking / four hook mistakes:** Kallaway. §3 of the playbook is the surviving synthesis.
- **Missed from the B2B source:** the visual hook is specified there as a **3-to-5 word** text overlay. The playbook states no word limit.

### `script-playbook.md`

Claims to be "adapted from a studied four-step scriptwriting system". Its four blockers are Interestingness, Hook, Story, Engagement.

**Timeline problem:** the playbook was written **2026-07-09**. The Kallaway script transcript was pasted **2026-07-11**, two days later. Its framework has **five steps plus a Step 0**, and it is not the four-blocker system the playbook describes. So the playbook's stated source is something else, and this transcript appears never to have been integrated at all.

Present in the transcript, absent from the playbook:

- **Step 0, Psychology: expectations vs reality.** The governing principle of the whole framework. When reality beats expectation the viewer stays; when expectation beats reality they leave. Nothing equivalent exists in the playbook.
- **Step 4, Body: the 2-1-3-4 method.** Put your **second-best** point first and your **best** point second. Rising perceived value creates an escalation pattern that holds the viewer; leading with your best trains them that value is decaying and it is safe to leave. Non-obvious and directly actionable.
- **Step 5, Outro: the fortune cookie.** Close by summarising and reminding the viewer of the pain you solved, so the last impression is that reality beat expectation. Named after the free chocolate a restaurant brings with the bill.
- **Step 3, Intro: a five-step formula.** Not yet extracted in detail.

Also missed, from the B2B source rather than Kallaway:

- **"But / Therefore" transitions, never "And then."** The causality rule that stops a script being a list of events.
- **Loop the close back to the hook** to drive rewatches. This overlaps with the fortune-cookie outro and with the four-move arc's closing question; the three need reconciling rather than stacking.

### Status of the gaps

**Recorded, not applied.** None of the above has been added to either playbook. Applying them is a separate decision, partly because the outro techniques (fortune cookie, loop-back, closing question) overlap and would otherwise pile three different closes into one script.
