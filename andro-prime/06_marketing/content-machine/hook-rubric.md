# Hook rubric

The grading standard every Andro Prime hook is scored against, whether an agent wrote it or Keith did. Adapted from Kallaway's illusion-of-novelty framework and hook machine (`sources/kallaway-frameworks.md`), rebuilt around this brand's honesty constraint.

**Why a rubric and not just a playbook.** A playbook tells you how to write one. A rubric tells you whether the one you wrote is any good, and why. Generating without grading produces a pile of plausible lines and no way to choose between them. The grading pass is where the quality comes from.

**Cold-start note.** Kallaway's version derives its criteria from the creator's own top-performing videos. Andro Prime has no video back catalogue yet, so v1 is derived from principle rather than data. Section 5 is the slot where learned criteria go once real numbers exist. Do not pretend to have data we do not have.

---

## 1. Hard gates (fail any = discard, no score)

Check these first. A hook that trips a gate is not rewritten into shape; it is thrown away and the topic is approached differently.

| Gate | Fails if |
| --- | --- |
| **Claims** | Contains diagnose / treat / cure / fix, asserts a supplement caused a change, implies TRT is available, names ashwagandha, or infers low T from a Kit 2 marker. `03_compliance/CONTEXT.md` is the authority. |
| **Real numbers** | Contains a bloodwork figure that is not Keith's actual result. Placeholders only: `[your real <marker> number]`. Never invent a number to make a line land. |
| **Truthful biography** | A first-person line asserts something about Keith's own history that is not evidenced. The avatar is who a hook is written **TO**, never who it is written **AS**: every "I" belongs to Keith and must be checkable against `author-bios.md` or a filed result. "I have never once been shown the actual sheet" scored 11/12 and was false of him (his GP ordered a panel in November 2025 and he saw it); Keith caught it with "who is this Mark guy? Mark is not me." Discard, do not rescore. The Real-numbers gate catches fabricated figures and nothing catches fabricated experience, which is the more damaging of the two because it cannot be corrected later without discrediting the speaker. (Observation 82.) |
| **Live marker** | Built on a marker no live kit measures. |
| **Truthful reveal** | The "new" angle is not actually true, or is true only if the viewer does not know it is old. See §3. |
| **Repeated opener** | Reuses an opener already in the library. Check `templates/hook-bank.md` and prior assets (`tone-of-voice.md` §3). |
| **Em dash** | Contains one. |

## 2. Scored dimensions (0 to 2 each, 12 total)

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **New reveal** | Restates what Mark already knows ("low testosterone makes you tired") | A mild reframe | A genuine new angle on the topic: a finding, a reframe, a named thing he has not heard put this way |
| 2 | **Outcome mapping** | No outcome, or an outcome Mark does not want | Outcome implied | Explicitly tied to certainty, energy, clarity, or being himself again. Dream outcome is **certainty, never a cure** |
| 3 | **Contrast framing** | No old belief named | Old belief vague | A specific belief Mark holds, held constant, with the new angle as its true opposite |

**Before scoring dimension 3, decide which Mark.** `avatar-mark.md` §C now separates **[power user]** beliefs (the normal-range wound, "they said I was fine") from **[mainstream]** ones (speed and access, billing distrust). They are different men with different starting beliefs, so a contrast aimed at the wrong one scores 2 on paper and lands on nobody. State which you targeted in the score line. Mainstream hooks lead on speed and access; the normal-range wound is the trust layer underneath, not the opener.
| 4 | **Specificity** | Vague qualifiers | One concrete element | Concrete throughout: a number, a marker, a named moment, a time |
| 5 | **Muted legibility** | Needs audio to make sense | Works muted but long | Reads in 3 to 5 words as an overlay and stops the scroll on its own |
| 6 | **Voice** | Town crier. "Huge news", "this changes everything" | Neutral | Gossip whisperer. Peer letting someone in on something, never Authority announcing |

**Threshold: 9/12 to surface.** Below 9, rewrite or discard. Never present a hook below threshold as an option, even to fill a set of three.

**Scoring is not a formality.** State the score and the per-dimension reason. A hook that scores 9 with a 0 on contrast framing is a different problem from one that scores 9 with 1s across the board, and the fix differs.

## 3. The honesty constraint (this brand's departure from the source)

Kallaway's step 5 says never reveal that your new angle is an old thing dressed up. **Andro Prime rejects that.** The positioning is separation of incentives and "we'd rather lose a sale"; a concealment rule is incompatible with it, and in health advertising presenting established science as new discovery is misleading by omission regardless of intent.

The replacement rule:

> **The reveal must survive being explained.** If a viewer learned tomorrow exactly how old the underlying science is, the hook must still be true and still land. If it only works while they do not know, it fails the truthful-reveal gate.

This costs less than it sounds. The brand's central reveal is genuinely new to the man hearing it and genuinely true: the reference range was built to flag illness, not to define wellness, so "within range" does not mean "well". Cited, checkable, and counter to what he walked in believing. There is no illusion to protect.

What is kept from step 5 is the **delivery half**: gossip whisperer, not town crier. That is dimension 6 and it is entirely compatible with honesty. Lowering your voice is a register, not a deception.

Where a topic has no honest new angle, do not manufacture one. `hook-playbook.md` §1 already gives the answer: do not make the video. There is always another idea.

## 4. The proof ladder (what the hook can promise the script will deliver)

From Kallaway's bullseye proof. Proof strength is a function of how close it sits to the viewer's own life, because people distrust third-party claims and trust versions of themselves.

| Rung | Proof | Andro Prime's position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A single person who mimics the viewer: same age, same problem, same situation | **Partly available.** Keith is the right demographic and his symptom experience is relatable. His *cause* is not typical, so his story can carry the feeling and never the mechanism. Never let it imply "what happened to me will happen to you". |
| 2 | The warm crowd. "A lot of men who test come back…" | **Not yet.** Opens up as customer results accumulate. This is the rung to plan for. |
| 3 | Third-party verification: a study, a stat, a guideline | **Where we live today.** NHS ranges, Wu et al., BSSM. Weakest rung, but honest and defensible. |

Climb as high as the truth allows and no higher. Kallaway's own caveat applies with more force here than in his category: if the proof is not there, you do not manufacture it.

A hook may **promise** the rung the script will deliver. It may not claim a rung the script cannot reach.

## 6. Grading a hook Keith wrote

The single most useful mode, and the one that needs no data. Given a draft:

1. Run the hard gates. A gate failure is reported as a gate failure, not a low score.
2. Score all six dimensions, with a one-line reason each.
3. State the weakest dimension and what specifically is missing.
4. Offer **three rewrites**, each fixing the weakest dimension while holding Keith's intent and wording where possible.
5. Never silently improve his line. Show the original, the score, and the alternatives, and let him choose.
