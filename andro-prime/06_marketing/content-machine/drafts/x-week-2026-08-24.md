**Post by hand.** Metricool will not split X copy into a thread, so this one is manual until that mechanism is tested. This is the plan's own rule (`x-channel-plan.md` §5), and `slot: by-hand` registers `publisher='manual'` so the shared scheduler refuses it by name rather than a reader having to remember.

**1/**
> Most men push through constant tiredness, then go looking for something to test for.
>
> There is a cheaper step in between that almost nobody runs properly.
>
> A thread on the fortnight test.

**2/**
> The premise first. For most healthy men in their late 30s and 40s the cause is not dramatic and not a hidden disease. It is the boring stuff, added up.
>
> The NHS list is the usual suspects: not enough good sleep, stress, low mood, too little activity, alcohol, caffeine.

**3/**
> You do not need a protocol. Pick two or three and hold them.
>
> The same wake time every day, weekends included, and a 30 minute wind-down with the screens off.
>
> Last coffee before 2pm.

**4/**
> Trim the week's drinking and put two drink-free nights in.
>
> Add a daily walk, ideally in daylight. If you have been pushing in the gym, add an easy week rather than another session.

**5/**
> Build breakfast around protein and fibre rather than fast carbs. A breakfast that is all fast carbs gives you a spike and then a slump that arrives, like clockwork, at 2pm.
>
> The boring basics are boring because they work.

**6/**
> Then the part that makes it a test rather than a resolution.
>
> A single tired week tells you nothing. A fortnight of honest changes tells you whether the cause is on this list.
>
> That is the whole method.

**7/**
> A month of honest changes and no movement, or tiredness that is now normal rather than an off patch, is a GP conversation.
>
> Sooner if it came on suddenly, or with weight loss, breathlessness, or low mood that will not lift.
>
