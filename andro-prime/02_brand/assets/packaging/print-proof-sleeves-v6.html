What changed from v5, and why

  • The window grew by 27.0 mm in width and moved 19.5 mm down. v5 reserved 81.0 × 51.5 mm at 49.0 mm from the left; the real rectangle is 108.00 × 57.00 mm at 34.09 mm. That is 47% more area.
  • v5's activation block collided with the real window. It sat at 11–32 mm across and 90–111 mm down, and the window occupies 47.5–104.5 mm down. The back face is re-laid-out into three bands: brand above the window, window clear, regulatory below.
  • The QR code is gone from the sleeve back. Per the /activate deprecation already recorded in 02_brand/STATE.md: the sampling QR belongs on the insert, and "activate your kit" becomes "see your results", because the kit is already linked to the customer at order creation.
  • Fronts are unchanged apart from being scaled to true size. Only the sleeve front is kit-specific; the back is universal across all three.

Two things this proof assumes

1. The fold is at the midpoint. Vitall's file carries no cut or crease layer — no spot colours, no custom colours, one page — so it is printed artwork, not a cutter guide. 152.00 mm is exactly half of 304.00 mm and the geometry supports it, but it has not been confirmed.

2. Only the back face carries a window. A rectangle of identical size sits on the other face in Vitall's artwork. If both faces need one, the hero front has a 108 × 57 mm hole in the middle of it and pages 2 to 4 need redrawing, not adjusting. This is the first question to put to Ben.

Typefaces are substituted in this proof (system serif / mono / sans standing in for Merriweather, JetBrains Mono and Inter). Geometry is exact; letterforms are not. Colour is screen RGB and has not been converted to CMYK.
