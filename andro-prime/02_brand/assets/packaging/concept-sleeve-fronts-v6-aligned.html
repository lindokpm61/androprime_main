Why this changes — the table is the brand

The old sleeve fronts and the chosen supplement bottle no longer belong to the same brand. The sleeve was centred type on a warm-white field; the bottle that won is a black field with a white channel carrying the wordmark, and a mono data table as the hero. This concept moves the sleeve onto the bottle's system.

The device transfers exactly, and for the same physical reason. On the bottle, the white channel is the bottle showing through the label. On the sleeve, the channel is the board showing through the black band — it runs off the top and bottom of the band into the clean board, so it is unmistakably the material and not a printed stripe.

The stronger link is the table. The supplement lists what it gives you, at a dose. The kit lists what it measures, in a unit. Same mono rows, same hairline rules, same right-aligned column. One typographic object across the whole range: Andro Prime is the brand that puts the numbers on the front of the box.

Notes and open items

What is carried over from the locked sleeve constraints

  • Dieline unchanged. Face 179 × 152 mm from Vitall's dieline. This is the front only; the back and the insert stay universal across kits, and the back must still leave the white cutout for Vitall's lot, expiry and compliance label. Nothing here touches that.
  • Credentials promoted to a legible front line, as the 2026-06-12 research direction requires: the UKAS line, the clinical sign-off line and the IVD / CE / UKCA marks all sit on the clean board strip at the foot, in black, at full legibility rather than as reversed small print on the black.
  • Only the front is kit-specific, so this remains three 500-unit runs plus one universal back and insert. The black band, channel and wordmark are identical across all three, so the plates differ only in the type block.
  • Kit 1 copy scope respected: "Find out if testosterone is the cause", never a general fatigue frame.

The decision this forces

🔴 The 2026-06-12 sleeve direction says black is the riskiest choice in a health frame, and chose a warm-white-led exterior on that basis. This concept puts a large black band on the front. It is a smaller departure than it looks, because the board still frames the band on three axes and the channel is bare board, but it is a departure and it should be decided knowingly, not absorbed.

Two honest ways to take it: accept the band, on the argument that the black is now doing structural brand work rather than decorating a health product, and that the credentials sit on white where they read best. Or invert the sleeve only — keep the board dominant and reduce the black to the channel and the table rules — which preserves the 2026-06-12 finding.
