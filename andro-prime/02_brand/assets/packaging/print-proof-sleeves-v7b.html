Sleeve print proof — Route B, warm white board

True size · built to Vitall's artwork spec · 23 August 2026

Palette variant. Ivory #F4F1EA board, #141414 ink. The research-led default: warm white rather than surgical white, on the grounds that pure white reads clinical in a health frame. The palette call is still open, so this proof exists in both; geometry and copy are identical between them.

Route B from concept-sleeve-fronts-v7-two-routes.html transposed onto the real dimensions from Vitall's artwork file. Fronts are lifted verbatim from that concept, with one copy change. The back is new: Route B had fronts only, so it has been drawn in the same system and laid out around the compliance window.

The one copy change

"Thresholds signed off by Dr Ewa Lindo, GMC-registered GP" is removed from all three fronts. Removed programmatically and asserted at exactly three matches, so it cannot have been missed on one kit. "ANALYSED IN A UKAS-ACCREDITED UK LABORATORY" stays.

Worth a decision rather than an assumption: the Ewa attribution still appears on the live site and in approved copy. Taking it off the pack only is fine, but if the intent is that it is not needed anywhere, that is a decision sweep, not a packaging edit.

What the aperture does to Route B

  • It does not touch the fronts. Route B's marker table, headline and spine are all on the front face, which carries no window (confirmed below).
  • The back is banded around it: brand and next step above, window clear, regulatory below. There is no room for anything else.
  • The spine flips to the right on the back in reading orientation, so it wraps the same physical edge as the front's after the fold.
  • No QR on the sleeve. Per the /activate deprecation the sampling QR belongs on the insert, and the kit is already linked at order creation.
It is a HOLE, not a printed panel (Keith, from a physical kit, 23 August). The board is die-cut; Vitall's kit-details label is stuck to the box underneath and shows through the aperture. That is why their print file leaves the area white and carries no cut layer — the die is a separate tool their kitting house holds. Matching the existing die exactly is what avoids new tooling cost.
Confirmed 23 August (Keith): only the back face carries an aperture. The front is clear, so Route B's marker table, headline and spine are unaffected and this proof stands as drawn. The identically sized rectangle on the other face of Vitall's own artwork is their lifestyle photo frame reusing the same dimensions, not a second compliance panel.

What is still open with Vitall

  • The fold line. Still assumed at the 152.00 mm midpoint. Their file has no cut or crease layer, so a real cutter guide is a fresh ask.
  • In-house printing. On 3 June Ben said a new kitting solution was coming "in the next few months" that would print in-house rather than take supplied sleeves. That is now due, and it may retire the 500-sleeve minimum before we commit to a run.
