Amended 25 August 2026 · decision sweep

The core argument stands and is unchanged. The shelf is identical for everyone, nothing on it is conditioned on a result, and the results tab carries no products. That was decision 2 of 22 August and the app-led decision of 25 August explicitly left it intact.

Three things on this page were stale and are corrected. Supplements are no longer a monthly subscription: they are a shop at member price, which is what Function and Superpower actually do, and it takes stock, dispatch cadence and the 3PL out of the membership. The three products shown were the old range and none of them is in the current one; they are kept below only so the layout reads, marked accordingly. And the monthly prices were built on a blended pack that cannot be manufactured.

One correction is more serious, and it is why the two-range card exists. Man B's card previously read "Below range" and "below the normal range for adult men" beside a printed reference interval of 8.64 to 29.00, with a value of 9.1. He is inside that interval. What he is below is our clinical action cutoff of 12, which is a different thing built for a different purpose. Telling a man he is below a range while showing him a range he is inside is wrong on the page and is the exact "moving the goalposts" charge the brand positions against. Both numbers are now drawn.

Why this fails. Nobody wrote a recommendation, but the page reads as one. The ASA judges the overall impression a reasonable consumer takes from the page, not what we intended by it. A product sitting directly under "your testosterone is low, see your GP" is read as an answer to that result. It is also the exact structural conflict the brand positions against.

What this actually is, mechanically

  • A top-level tab on the dashboard, not a new page and not a popup. One extra click, in the nav he is already looking at, in the same place for every customer.
  • Nothing on it is conditioned on a result. Same products, same order, same copy, whether he is all clear, borderline or GP-routed. That is what makes "no result changes what we offer" true rather than argued.
  • The results tab carries no products at all. Not suppressed for some men and shown to others, which was the old maintenance-offer shape. Absent for everyone, so there is no rule to get wrong.
  • It solves the stale-reading problem. A first low reading that turns out to be timing never suppresses anything, because nothing was ever conditioned on it. The man sees the same shelf before and after his recheck.
  • It reuses what is already built. The kit-tab component, the card system and the waitlist route all exist. The maintenance-offer CTA branch comes out.
  • Member price, bought one at a time. Not a subscription, and not included in the membership. Function and Superpower both run a member-priced marketplace on top of a fee that already covers far more than ours does; including supplements would be more generous than the market leaders and it is the part carrying the physical cost.
The one carve-out still to put to Ewa

Vitamin D above the assay ceiling. Her 2026-08-07 upper-band ruling suppresses every supplement CTA on a GP-block card, with the reason recorded in the classifier: the last thing a man over 250 should be shown is more D3. On a shelf that is identical for everyone, that man still sees a 4,000 IU D3 product. This is a product-safety exclusion tied to one marker and one product, not a result-conditional shelf, and it is the version worth putting to her.
