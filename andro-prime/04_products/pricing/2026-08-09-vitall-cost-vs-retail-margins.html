What the kit price includes, and what to watch

  • The lab price is all-in per completed order. It covers lab analysis, standard reporting, agreed result delivery and standard home-kit logistics (Schedule 1 §1.3, §3). Zero-rated for VAT as a health service.
  • A cancelled order is arguably not billable. Billing is per completed order and an order only becomes non-cancellable once the lab has processed it. There is no stated cancellation fee before that point, though there is also no cancel endpoint, so it is a manual request.
  • Vitall can raise these prices on 30 days' notice where the rise traces to sub-processor, legal, logistics or consumables costs (§10). We may terminate rather than accept.
  • Refunds come entirely out of our margin. Any customer refund or compensation is Andro Prime's responsibility; Vitall never reimburses it.
  • Kit 2 is the best-margin kit, not Kit 3. Kit 3 earns the most cash per sale (£81.00) but Kit 2 keeps the largest share of its price (47.1%).
  • Affiliate sales on Kit 1 are close to marginal. £13.37 on a £89.10 sale is 15%, and a single replacement kit at £12 would take almost all of it.
