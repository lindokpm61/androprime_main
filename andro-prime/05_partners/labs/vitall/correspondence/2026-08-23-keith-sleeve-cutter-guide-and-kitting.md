  reviewing a version we then supersede. Ask, finalise, then send.

## What changed our understanding, and why it drives the email

Keith checked a physical kit on 23 August: **the details panel is die-cut out of the sleeve**,
and Vitall's label is stuck to the box underneath and shows through the hole. It is not a printed
white area. Three consequences, all of which shaped the ask:

1. It explains why their `.eps` carries no cut or crease layer. That file is the printed artwork;
   the die is a separate tool their kitting house holds.
2. **A cutter guide stops being optional.** You cannot specify a hole without one.
3. **A die already exists**, so matching it exactly is what avoids tooling cost. That is why the
   email offers to match rather than asking them to accommodate us. Full working:
   `../../../02_brand/assets/packaging/dieline/README.md`.

**Corner radius is ours, not theirs.** 3.2 mm was measured off a raster export (12 px on the left
edge, 13 px on the top, at 3.7801 px/mm). A die always carries a radius because sharp internal
corners tear, and the real value is almost certainly a round 3.0 or 3.5. The email says "roughly"
for that reason. **Do not print to 3.2 on our own measurement.**
