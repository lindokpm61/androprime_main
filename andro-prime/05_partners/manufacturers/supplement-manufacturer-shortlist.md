**Manufacturer must be able to handle:** capsule encapsulation (Daily Stack) AND powder blending with sachet filling or tub filling (Collagen). Ideally one manufacturer for both, but two separate is acceptable if capabilities don't overlap.

## Critical Note — MOQ vs Gate 0A Volume Gap

Gate 0A requires 25 pre-orders. That translates to roughly:

- **Daily Stack (V7.2):** 25 × 60 capsules = 1,500 capsules. Most custom capsule MOQs start at 25,000-30,000. Note: V7.2 reformulation reduces total active ingredient mass per capsule (magnesium 400mg removed), meaning the capsule fill is lighter — likely fits comfortably in size 0 capsule rather than needing size 00. Confirm capsule sizing with manufacturer.
- **Joint & Recovery Collagen:** 25 × 30 sachets = 750 sachets (~8.25kg of powder). Sachet filling MOQs are typically higher due to form-fill-seal setup costs.

This means either:

1. Ordering significantly more than pre-orders justify (cash tied up in inventory), or
2. Finding a manufacturer who genuinely works at startup scale (Tier 1 below), or
3. Starting with a stock/private-label formulation close to the Daily Stack spec, then switching to full custom once volumes justify it

**Additional complexity:** sachet filling is a more specialised capability than tub filling. Not every powder manufacturer has sachet lines. If sachet is preferred over tub, this must be an explicit question in outreach. Tub filling is more universally available and may have lower MOQs.

This gap must be resolved before committing to a manufacturer.

## Questions to Ask All Manufacturers

Adapted from the Vitall outreach brief approach — extract comparable data from each.

**Product 1 — Daily Stack V7.2 (capsules):**
1. What is your MOQ for a custom 4-ingredient capsule formulation? (Zinc Gluconate equivalent to 25mg elemental zinc, Vitamin D3 Cholecalciferol 4,000 IU, Methylcobalamin B12 1,000mcg, Ashwagandha KSM-66 600mg)
2. What capsule sizes can you produce? Total active ingredient mass per serving (2 capsules) is approximately 800–900mg, so ~400–450mg per capsule — should fit comfortably in size 0. Confirm.
3. Can you source KSM-66 ashwagandha extract? (Required ingredient — do not disclose marketing restrictions around it)
4. Can you source pure Methylcobalamin B12 (active form, not cyanocobalamin or a blend)? Confirm USP grade or equivalent. **Open question with Andro Prime: should B12 dose increase from 1,000mcg to 5,000mcg given "Active B12" positioning?** Quote both options if practical.
5. Stability testing requirement: V7.2 is a new formulation (V7.1 had Magnesium, which is removed). V7.1 stability data does not transfer. What is your cost and timeline for V7.2 stability testing?

**Product 2 — Joint & Recovery Collagen (powder):**
4. What is your MOQ for a custom powder blend (~11g per serving, 5 ingredients including 10g bovine hydrolysed collagen peptides)?
5. Do you offer sachet filling (single-serve, form-fill-seal)? If so, what is the MOQ for sachets specifically vs tub filling?
6. Can you source UC-II undenatured collagen (chicken sternum, Type II)?

**Both products:**
7. Can you handle both capsule encapsulation and powder blending/filling under one agreement, or are they separate production lines?
8. What is your lead time from approved formulation to first batch delivered?
9. Do you provide formulation support, or do we need to supply a complete spec?
10. What certifications do you hold? (GMP, BRC, HACCP, MHRA, NSF, organic)
11. Do you handle packaging, labelling, and bottling/filling, or is that separate?
12. What is your pricing structure — per unit, per batch, setup fees?
13. What are your payment terms — deposit, net 30, etc.?
14. Do you offer storage and fulfilment / drop-shipping, or manufacturing only?
15. What is the minimum contract length or commitment?
16. Can you provide samples or a trial batch before committing to full production?
17. Are you UK-based manufacturing only, or do you outsource any steps?
