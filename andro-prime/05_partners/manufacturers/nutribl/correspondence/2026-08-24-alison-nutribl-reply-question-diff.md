## What the two answers change

**Artwork is free, so the design tier is now optional.** The call sheet's recommendation was the CLASSIC 5-slot
Starter Bundle at GBP 145, chosen because it undercut three individual GBP 140 setups and left two spare slots.
That reasoning still holds *if we buy design*. It is no longer the only route: supplying finished artwork to
their published spec costs nothing, and it also removes the **GBP 109 vector-source-file charge (SER116)**, which
was the one place we would not have owned our own label artwork. Given the sleeve work is already in-house, the
default should flip to supplying our own files, with the bundle as the fallback if the spec turns out to be
fussier than it reads.

**We are the FBO, which was assumed and is now stated.** Consequences: our business name and a UK address must
appear on every bottle, the claims printed on it are our legal responsibility rather than Nutribl's, and their
existing disclaimer that label content is "purely advisory and ultimate responsibility lies with the brand
owners" is consistent with that rather than something to argue with. This is what makes question 2 the gate it
is: we carry the liability for wording we did not yet establish we can control.
