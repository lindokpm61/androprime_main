## Why each question is there

| Ask | Why it is worth the email |
|---|---|
| 3PL application | Longest external chain behind it: they assess, they send the outline, we open an account with their partner, only then can stock ship there. Starting it first is what shortens the calendar |
| Custom cart interface | Their integrations list is a fixed set of platforms and ours is not on it. This is the one place the 3PL model does not slot into our build for free |
| Own artwork | Vector source files cost £109 extra (SER116), so artwork they design is not ours unless we pay again. Supplying our own sidesteps it |
| Claim selection | Their own listings print unauthorised lines, notably "Turmeric may help to support healthy liver function" on five SKUs. If we cannot omit those, private label with them is a compliance problem, not a cost question |
| FBO | The FBO answers to Trading Standards for the label. On stock private label it is normally the brand owner, so probably us. Nothing in the repo establishes it, and it is cheaper to know before stock is printed than after |
