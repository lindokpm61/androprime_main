## Compliance position (state this early, do not leave it to the label stage)

> "We are targeting a 500 to 1,500 unit first batch, scaling to roughly 5,000 units a month within six months. Is there a reduced unit price on the first batch in exchange for a commitment to scale through your facility through Year 1?"
