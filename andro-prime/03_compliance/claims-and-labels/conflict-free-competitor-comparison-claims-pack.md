## 1. Why this cluster, and why it needs its own pack

The frustration the whole strategy is built on searches Google as **distrust of the treatment funnels**, not as "cost": `numan reviews` 1,300/mo (KD 14), `thriva reviews` 880 (KD 7), `medichecks reviews` 390 (KD 2), `is numan legit` 260 (KD 10), `is thriva worth it` 170 (KD 9), plus clean comparison heads (`home blood test uk` 260/KD 7). Roughly 2.5-3.5k/mo of high commercial intent at KD 2-14, and it is the purest keyword expression of the conflict-free position. It also feeds GEO: we are cited **0 times across 48 LLM answers** today, and LLMs lean on comparison pages + Reddit.

It needs its own pack because **naming a competitor is the highest legal-risk copy the business produces.** Three regimes bite at once:

1. **ASA / CAP comparative-advertising rules (CAP Code s.3.33-3.45).** Comparisons with identifiable competitors must compare products meeting the same need, be based on **verifiable** facts, compare **material and representative** features, and must **not mislead, denigrate, or take unfair advantage** of a competitor's name.
2. **Defamation Act 2013 + malicious falsehood.** Any false, damaging statement about a named company is actionable. Every competitor fact must be **true, current, and substantiated on file** before it is published.
3. **Our own honesty limits (from the mainstream research).** The verified public facts are narrow; the temptation to overreach ("they sell your data", "it's a scam") is exactly what turns a defensible comparison into a liability.

The pack defines the say / don't-say so every `/compare/` asset can be pre-flighted and shipped without re-litigating the boundary each time.

## 2. The proposed frame (one line)

**Honest orientation, not a hit piece.** Help the man choosing a men's blood test understand the market honestly, show where each option's incentives sit as a factual, verifiable observation, and position Andro Prime as the men's brand with no treatment to sell on the back of a low result. We describe business models; we never call a competitor a fraud.

## 3. The honesty angle (why being straight is also the safe play)

The mainstream complaint about the funnels is **billing distrust, not anti-TRT ideology**: verified 1-star clusters about undisclosed paid follow-up tests and buried subscriptions, and a live **ASA ruling against Numan** for it. We do not need to exaggerate any of that. Stating the funnel model plainly (sell a cheap entry test, monetise a "low" result with a treatment subscription) is **true, so it clears ASA substantiation**, and it is the whole differentiator. The precise, defensible position:

- We lead with the **wallet** (one price, nothing hidden, we earn the same whether you are low or fine), not with an attack on testosterone treatment. TRT is a legitimate treatment; our point is the **conflict of interest at the point of testing**, not that the treatment is bad.
- The conflict-free claim differentiates us **from the treatment funnels** (Numan, Voy, Manual, Hims), **not** from the honest-broker generalists (Thriva, Forth, Medichecks), who are also conflict-free. Against the generalists the differentiator is **men's-specific + our test design**, never "conflict-free" (see §7, C4: do not claim to be "the only conflict-free test").

## 4. Proposed APPROVED language (Ewa to redline)

Draft customer-facing lines. No em dashes (repo hook + house rule). Ewa edits or strikes any line; only signed lines may be used.

**The conflict-free claim (reuse the CA-026-approved "separation of incentives" form, never the absolute):**
- "The men's testosterone test with no reason to sell you testosterone."
- "One price, nothing hidden. If your testosterone is low we point you to your GP, and we earn the same whether your result is low or completely fine."
- "We sell the test, not what happens after a bad number. There is no treatment subscription waiting at the end of your result."

> Do NOT use "we will never sell testosterone" or "we will never offer treatment": it is false post-CQC. The durable form is the **separation of incentives at the point of testing** (no upsell rides on your result), which stays true when the clinical service launches. Same rule as CA-026.

**Describing the treatment-funnel model (factual, non-denigrating):**
- "Some men's-health brands sell a low-cost testosterone test and make their money from the treatment a low result leads to. That is a legitimate business, but it means the same company that reads your result also profits if it is low."
- "Numan, Voy and Manual sell a testosterone test and, for a low result, a TRT subscription. We do not offer TRT, so a low result earns us nothing extra."

**Citing the ASA ruling (the one hard public fact, cite precisely):**
- "In 2022 the Advertising Standards Authority ruled a Numan test ad misleading (ruling A22-1153049): the ad's refund promise applied to only a small share of customers and the ongoing subscription was not made clear." (Writer verifies the exact wording against the published ruling at draft time; state only what the published ruling says.)

**The data point (positive commitment + structural observation ONLY, never an accusation):**
- "We are one UK data controller, and we do not sell your data." (positive commitment about us.)
- "A business that makes money from treatment has a commercial reason to hold on to health and symptom data. We do not." (structural observation, framed about the model, not an assertion that a named company sells data.)

**Comparison-table lines (factual, dated, verifiable):**
- Each competitor cell states only a fact that is **currently true and on file** in the substantiation register (§8), with an "as of [month/year]" stamp. Prices, subscription terms, and "what they sell" are verified at draft and re-checked before publish.

**GP-referral / boundary line (unchanged from house rule):**
- "If your testosterone comes back low, that is a conversation with your GP, not a product we sell you."

## 5. Proposed BANNED language (Ewa to confirm / extend)

Must not appear in any `/compare/` asset (hub, manifesto, FAQ, table, social, or GEO derivative). Inherits the CONTEXT.md red-flag table.

- "scam" / "fraud" / "con" / "rip-off" / "dodgy" / "sham" applied to any named competitor (defamation + ASA denigration).
- "[Competitor] sells your data" or any assertion, stated as fact, that a named company sells or misuses personal data (unverified: malicious-falsehood risk). Use the §4 positive-commitment + structural-observation forms only.
- "we will never sell testosterone" / "we will never offer treatment" (false post-CQC; use the separation-of-incentives form).
- "the only conflict-free test" / "the only honest test" (false: the generalist brokers are also conflict-free; overclaim + ASA).
- Any statement that TRT / testosterone therapy is harmful, dangerous, unnecessary, or a con (we do not attack the treatment, only the conflict at the point of testing).
- Any competitor fact (price, subscription term, marker count, what they sell) that is not verified, dated, and in the §8 register. No "from memory" figures.
- Anything implying Andro Prime offers, will soon offer, or gives access to TRT / treatment (Phase 0 boundary). Comparison content routes to the **test**, never a treatment pathway.
- Anything implying endorsement, affiliation, or partnership with a named competitor.
- "diagnose" / "treat" / "cure" / "you have low T" (CONTEXT.md red-flag table).
- The FM list as a CTA (content never routes to the FM list).
- "Ashwagandha" / KSM-66 / withania somnifera, anywhere, ever (silent-ingredient rule).

## 6. Competitor-naming rules (the comparative-advertising checklist)

Every asset that names a competitor must satisfy all of the following before it goes to Ewa:

1. **Same-need comparison only.** Compare like with like (an at-home men's testosterone/blood test with another at-home test), not our test against their treatment.
2. **Verifiable + dated.** Every competitor fact traces to the §8 register with an "as of" date and a source (screenshot / URL). Re-verified at publish, because prices and terms move. Note the teardown already found the market shifts fast (Voy has moved toward GLP-1 weight-loss; prices change).
3. **Material + representative.** Compare features that matter to the buyer (price transparency, what happens on a low result, subscription vs one-off, accreditation), not cherry-picked trivia.
4. **Model, not motive.** Describe what a company **does** (its business model, verifiable), never impute intent ("they want to trap you"). The funnel conflict is a structural fact; a company's motives are not ours to assert.
5. **No denigration.** Neutral, factual register. The reader should conclude; the copy should not name-call.
6. **Generalists handled fairly.** Thriva / Forth / Medichecks are conflict-free too. Where they appear, the honest differentiator is men's-specific focus and our test design, not conflict-free. Do not imply they share the funnels' conflict.
