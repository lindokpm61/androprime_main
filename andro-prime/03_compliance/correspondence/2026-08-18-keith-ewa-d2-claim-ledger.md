usual shape and there was no artefact to attach it to. It still changes the compliance trail for
every derivative we publish from here on, which makes it exactly the class of approval the CA-028
lesson was written about: an approval whose text is not captured against the question asked cannot
later be told apart from an approval that never saw the question.

## What each ruling settles

**Ruling 1 (A): the claim-ledger model is adopted.** D2 is ruled. She signs a versioned, dated claim
set; derivatives that repeat those claims pin to a version and do not return to her; anything adding
an unsigned claim comes to her fresh.

**Ruling 2 (B): the claim set sits at the TOPIC, not the article.** This overrides plan step 5.1,
which was written as "Ewa signs a versioned claim set **at the article**". One set covers every
article on the same subject.

🔴 **"Topic" is not yet defined in our terms and must not be assumed to mean "pillar".** The word was
hers, offered as an option in a one-word answer, and our nearest existing unit is the content pillar.
They may be the same thing; nothing on the record says so. **This is a one-line question to her, and
it is owed before anything is built**, because the answer decides what the pin points at and
therefore the shape of the store.

**Ruling 3 (A): a reworded claim that adds nothing is covered by the pin.** A change of tense, or one
sentence split into two, does not break the pin. This generalises the CA-029 Amendment 1 of
2026-08-16 from a Keith-only ruling about one bio line into a standing clinical rule, and it is now
**Ewa's** ruling rather than Keith's reading of hers. The boundary is unchanged and was stated to her
in the question: **anything that adds a proposition still needs a fresh submission.**

**Ruling 4 (A): the principle is ruled once across both pending packets.** `869ejbq1d` (fatigue,
E1–E5) and `869ejcxp0` (inflammatory, G1–G5) no longer need separate answers.
