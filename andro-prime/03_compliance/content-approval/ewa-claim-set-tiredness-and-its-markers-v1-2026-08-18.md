- **Q11 (A):** she signs **a list we draft**, one sentence per claim with its source, rather than prose.

**What signing this does.** Derivatives (LinkedIn posts, Facebook posts, carousels, scripts, Substack
issues) pin to the signed set instead of coming to her one at a time. Tier 1, inherited verbatim or
reworded with nothing added, then auto-passes with no Ewa at all (**Q14 A**), which is where the time
saving lives.

**What signing this does NOT do.** It does not re-open or re-sign the four articles. **Q10 (A) is
forward only**: her existing sign-offs on all four stand exactly as given, and nothing here asks her to
revisit them. This set is the derivative-facing ledger, drawn from copy she has already cleared.

**Versioning, as she ruled it.** A new version is forced **only by a change that alters meaning**
(**Q12 B**); a rewording that does not move a claim does not. Live derivatives pinned to a superseded
set **keep running** and are re-pinned at their next edit (**Q13 A**). The set does **not expire**
(**Q15 A**); it is re-checked when we notice a cited source has moved.
