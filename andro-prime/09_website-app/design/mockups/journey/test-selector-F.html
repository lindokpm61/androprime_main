Selector logic

Hormone-led symptoms tend toward Kit 1. Recovery, inflammation, and training-load issues tend toward Kit 2. If the picture is mixed or you want the broadest starting point, the selector defaults upward to Kit 3.

On the live page this is a bordered slab with a floating question mark in the corner. Here it is a tray, because it is the page explaining its own reasoning, which is exactly the kind of thing F puts in a tray.
