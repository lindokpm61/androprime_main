Ask the clinician · answered this month

Why does vitamin D take so long to shift?

Stored vitamin D turns over slowly, so blood levels respond over weeks rather than days. Most people testing after supplementation see a meaningful change somewhere between eight and sixteen weeks, which is why retesting sooner usually tells you very little.
