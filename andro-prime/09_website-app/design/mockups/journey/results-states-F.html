What the lab is doing right now

Why analysis takes days, not hours

Your sample is run in a batch against calibrated controls, and a result that fails its control is run again rather than sent out. The wait is the second run you hope you do not need.

What a reference range is, and is not

A reference range describes where most men sit. It was built to flag illness, not to define wellness, which is why we will show ours beside it.
