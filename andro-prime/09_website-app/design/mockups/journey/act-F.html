The form is used on /supplements, on both product pages and on /supplement-waitlist, so a change to any state lands in four places at once. The `already` state is the one worth noticing: it is a distinct branch with its own heading, and it exists because re-joining a waitlist is the commonest thing a returning visitor does. Most forms treat that as success and lie slightly; this one does not.

Success takes the accent and `already` does not. Both replace the form, both say the same paragraph. Same reasoning as the message and error banners on the auth card: the accent goes on the state the reader did not already know about.
