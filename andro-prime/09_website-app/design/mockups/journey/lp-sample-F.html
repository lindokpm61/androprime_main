THE VIDEO IS DRAWN AS ABSENT, ON PURPOSE. The inventory records that this page needs a how-to-sample film and that no such film exists, which makes this page product design as much as redraw. A mockup that showed a video frame with a play button would be asserting there is a video, and the next reader would scope the build assuming one. An empty slot that says why it is empty is the only honest way to draw a dependency that has not been made.

The five steps

1 01

Wash your hands

Wash with warm water for 30 seconds to increase blood flow. Dry completely.

2 02

Use the lancet

Twist off the cap. Press it firmly against the side of your fingertip until you hear a click.

3 03

Fill the tube

Gently squeeze your finger. Wipe away the first drop, then fill the tube to the top line. Massage from palm to fingertip if slow.

4 04

Seal and pack

Snap the lid closed tightly. Place the tube in the biohazard bag, then into the original return box.

5 05

Post today

Use the pre-paid return envelope. Drop it in a priority postbox before the last collection of the day.
