Six directions, one page.

Same content, same compliance rails, same sourced data. Only the visual language changes, so what you are comparing is aesthetics and not six different arguments. All six are phone-first: open them on your phone, not just here. To flip between them live, open the picker and press 1 to 6, or the arrow keys.

A. Specimen

Bone / serif / photographic

Spends everything on photography and scale. The page is a sequence of full-bleed plates with type set into them, and the readout is presented the way a museum presents a specimen: framed, numbered, given room. The header is a photograph under a slow drift.

C. Broadsheet

Newsprint / halftone / printed

Spends everything on texture. A halftone screen sits over the whole page, the photographs are halftoned rather than reproduced, and the type is set in justified columns with a drop cap. The header is masthead rules drawing across. It argues like a piece of writing.

D. Machined

Paper / grotesk / depth

The other three answer flatness with structure. This one answers it with light and depth. Every card is a tray holding an inset core, every track is a recessed well, and the header carries motion instead of a photograph.

E. Machined / Cast

D, with a filmed ground

A variation on D that changes only the hero. Four seconds of soft light moving across a pale machined surface, ping-ponged so it loops with no seam. Takes the direction's own metaphor literally: the ground behind the type is the material the objects are cut from. Dark mode inverts the same file.

F. Machined / Field

D, with a filmed table and data over it

Two layers. Underneath, an ordinary kitchen table on the morning the post arrived: a mug, a sheet of paper under reading glasses, a draught lifting its corner, light moving across pale wood. What is held constant. The copy, the section order, the conflict-free receipt, and every number in the results panel, which is sourced line by line from 04_products/results-engine/thresholds.md. No direction sells or prices the membership. Zero em dashes anywhere.

What each direction is allowed to change. Palette family, type, density, layout families and the job the results panel does. No two directions reuse a palette, a display face, or a layout family, so they cannot converge into the same page wearing four coats of paint. E and F are the exception and are meant to be: they are D with one surface swapped, so the only thing to judge between them is the hero ground.
