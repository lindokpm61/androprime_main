Direction F: Machined / Field

A variation on D, changing one thing. Everything below the header is D unaltered: same trays and inset cores, same bento, same Geist, same tokens, same copy, same cited numbers. D was the direction Keith picked, so the point of E and F is to test one surface rather than relitigate the whole page.

What changes: the hero ground is a film of the actual thing, with the data over it. Two layers. Underneath, an ordinary kitchen table on the morning the post arrived: a mug of tea, a single sheet of paper with a pair of reading glasses resting on it, and light moving slowly across pale wood. A faint draught lifts the corner of the sheet and lets it settle. No people, no hands, no clinic, no visible blood, and nothing readable on the page. Over it, the canvas field from the same six markers the readout uses: real lab bands, real action bands, real marker positions, all from thresholds.md, abstracted until they read as texture, drifting at three depths. The picture says where you are; the field says what we do there.

The colour treatment is doing contrast work, not decoration. The footage as shot does not belong on this page: it is warm and mid-toned, and this page is near-white with near-black type. It is graded to monochrome and lifted, then carries a directional scrim rather than a flat one, heaviest at the left where the headline sits and lightest at the right where the objects are. That is what lets the type clear 4.5:1 while the picture still reads. Dark theme darkens the same file rather than inverting it, because inverting a real scene turns a pale table black and the objects white, which looks like a negative rather than a night kitchen.

Variance engine roll

  • Vibe: Soft Structuralism, which is the skill’s own consumer and health archetype. White and silver-grey ground, massive grotesk, highly diffused ambient shadow.
  • Layout: The Asymmetrical Bento. Twelve-column at 900px and up, single-column stack below it.

The header, per section 6

No longer non-photographic, which is the point of the variation. Still no scroll listener. The staggered mask reveal is unchanged from D. The canvas draws a single static frame under prefers-reduced-motion and the video pauses to a frame, which needed JS because CSS has no lever for video. Without JS at all the video still autoplays and the plain CSS rule from D stands in for the canvas, so the header is never an empty box.

The cost

709 KB for the 15.5s loop, at half the speed it was generated at. Poster frame is 98 KB and is taken from the loop’s own first frame, so there is no jump when the video takes over. The source carried an audio track; it is stripped, so the file is silent. Assets in assets/f/.

What is on the table, and where it stops

A mug, a sheet of paper under a pair of reading glasses, and light. The sheet is deliberately illegible: soft grey lines of type, no letterhead, no logo, no heading, no numbers, no readable words. That is the compliance line and it is a real one. An unbranded, unreadable letter on a kitchen table makes no claim; it is just post nobody has dealt with. A legible letterhead, anything reading as a named lab or the NHS, or any readable figures would imply a clinical service and a per-customer result, which the compliance list forbids outright.

How the loop closes

Ping-pong is out, because steam and a lifting page both have an arrow of time. A crossfade alone was not enough either: a fluttering page never returns to where it started, so dissolving the tail over the head showed two sheets at once. The fix is to make the clip return on its own, by passing the same still as both start and end frame. The paper then comes back to within 2.7 of frame zero, against 11.4 for the best crossfade point without it, and half a second of dissolve hides the rest.

Why this loop is a crossfade and E’s is a ping-pong

Ping-ponging a clip, forward then reversed, gives a perfect seam for nothing, which is why E uses it: light raking across a surface looks the same run backwards. It fails here. Steam rising is irreversible motion, and reversed it sucks back into the cup. So this clip plays forward only, with the last 1.5 seconds dissolved back over the first 1.5, which hides the seam while every frame still runs the right way. Ping-pong is only ever valid when nothing in frame has a direction.

Photography enters lower down, in the bento cells, exactly as in D.

What it departs from, and why

  • Radius. 28px squircles with a concentric 22px inner core. The double-bezel is the largest single contributor to the page not reading flat.
  • Shadow. Very large, very low-opacity ambient shadow, never a hard drop shadow.
  • Typeface. Geist, not Inter. high-end-visual-design bans Inter outright, and keeping it would have made D a version of B. It also retires the one impeccable finding v1 could not clear.
  • Ground. A soft luminance wash and fixed film grain instead of flat white. No colour gradient, no glow.
  • One accent. Amber, used only as the fill of the borderline chip, with near-black text on it. Red stays reserved for the results dashboard GP-block state.

Compliance

Zero em dashes, code comments included. No “diagnose”, “treat” or “cure”. The conflict-free receipt stated as written. No membership sold or priced. No EFSA-claim ingredient named, no ashwagandha, no TRT implied or available. No per-customer interpretation. Every value, band edge and marker position is computed from 04_products/results-engine/thresholds.md with the arithmetic written out in a comment beside it.
