Two of these sit where a standard report would say normal and stop. On the action bands our GP approved, they read borderline. That is not a diagnosis. It is the context a bare number does not carry.

“In range” is a statistical band, not a health band.

A reference range describes where most men sit. It exists to identify clinical deficiency: the threshold at which you are officially recognised as ill. That is a useful line, and it is not the same line as being well.

Active B12 is the plainest case. The assay calls anything above 37.5 normal, while the NICE guideline our GP follows treats 25 to 70 as an indeterminate zone. Same number, two verdicts.

Ferritin behaves the same way at the bottom of its range. The lab is content anywhere above 30. Our bands call 30 to 100 indeterminate, because that is the span where a man can feel exactly as described and still be told nothing is wrong.

None of this is a diagnosis, and none of it is a second opinion on your lab. It is the same number read against a second line, and the argument is only that you should be shown both.

A number is a fact. A record is an answer.

Direction C: Broadsheet

The axis is texture and print. The brief names four levers for answering “flat and barren”: photography, scale, texture and motion. This direction spends everything on texture. A halftone screen sits over the whole page, the photographs are halftoned rather than reproduced, the type is set in justified columns with a drop cap, and the readout is presented as a numbered figure the way a newspaper presents a chart. It argues like a piece of writing, because that is what it is.

Header

Type and rule, in motion, with no photograph and no light effect. The masthead rules draw across from the left, the dateline sets, the headline arrives, then the standfirst. Type is the section 6 lever this direction pulls, which is why the header carries no image at all.

How it cannot converge with A, B or D

  • Palette: cool screened newsprint. A is warm bone, B is near-black, D is white.
  • Display face: Instrument Serif, used nowhere else. A is Newsreader, a screen-first transitional, which reads nothing like a high-contrast display cut at this size.
  • Layout family: multi-column justified article with a drop cap, halftone plates and a classified-style kit spread. Used nowhere else.
  • Header lever: type and rule. A uses imagery, B uses data, D uses light.

A known finding, kept deliberately

The halftone screen is a repeating radial gradient, which the Impeccable detector flags as decorative surface patterning. It is kept, because it is the entire texture argument of this direction rather than an ornament on top of one. The same call the previous round made about the brand typeface.

Compliance

Zero em dashes, code comments included. No “diagnose”, “treat” or “cure”. The conflict-free receipt stated as written. No membership sold or priced. No EFSA-claim ingredient named, no ashwagandha, no TRT implied or available. Every value, band edge and marker position is computed from 04_products/results-engine/thresholds.md with the arithmetic written out beside it.
