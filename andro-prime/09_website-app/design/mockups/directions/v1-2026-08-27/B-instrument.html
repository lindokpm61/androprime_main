Direction B: Instrument

The idea: the site looks like the product rather than like a brochure about the product. The result readout is not an illustration beside the headline, it is the hero. Everything else on the page is arranged around that one object. This is the direction that says "we are a measuring instrument", and it is the furthest from a wellness brand.

Dials

DESIGN_VARIANCE 6   MOTION_INTENSITY 5   VISUAL_DENSITY 6. Density is the highest of the three directions because the data is the argument here.

How it is deliberately not Direction A

  • No serif anywhere. A is serif-led. B earns its difference from structure and density instead, so the two cannot be confused.
  • Cold palette, not bone. Smoke and silver-grey. The skill's palette-rotation rule says the warm packaging beige is spent once, in A, and must not be repeated.
  • Dark by default, with a cool light variant. A is light by default.
  • Different layout families: full-bleed data hero, scroll-snap kit rail, stepped timeline. No layout family is reused from A.

Motion, and why each piece of it exists

One device only: a staggered rise-on-enter, built with CSS scroll-driven animation (animation-timeline: view()), so there is no scroll listener and no JavaScript on the main thread. It communicates hierarchy: the readout arrives first, the argument follows. It collapses to static under prefers-reduced-motion. No marquee, no pinning, no parallax, because none of them would be communicating anything.

Compliance

Zero em dashes. No "diagnose", "treat", "cure". Conflict-free receipt verbatim. No membership sold or priced. No EFSA-claim ingredients, no ashwagandha, no TRT availability. All values and band positions from thresholds.md. Not pre-flighted yet.
