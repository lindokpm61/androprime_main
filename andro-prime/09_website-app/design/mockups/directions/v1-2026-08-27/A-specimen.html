"In range" is a statistical band, not a health band.

A reference range describes where most men sit. It exists to identify clinical deficiency: the threshold at which you are officially recognised as ill. That is a useful line, and it is not the same line as being well.

Two ranges. Nine markers. You should see both, on all of them.

It is true of every marker, not one of them. Active B12 is the plainest case: the assay calls anything above 37.5 normal, while the NICE guideline our GP follows treats 25 to 70 as an indeterminate zone. Same number, two verdicts, and a standard report only ever shows you one of them.

Direction A: Specimen

The idea: the website is the packaging. Your kit boxes are already warm bone stock with a black spine, a serif product name, hairline rules and mono data. This direction takes that language literally and makes the spine architectural: a fixed black rail down the left edge on desktop that lies down into the top bar on a phone. Nothing here is invented. It is your own box, unfolded.

Dials

DESIGN_VARIANCE 5   MOTION_INTENSITY 3   VISUAL_DENSITY 4. Low motion is deliberate: regulated health is a quiet-constraint brief and the skill says those constraints override aesthetic preference.

Where it departs from brand-guidelines v2.0, and why

  • Bone, not white. v2.0 says white background. The packaging is not white and .blog-skin already documents cream #f4f4f0 as an approved surface. This follows the packaging.
  • Serif display. v2.0 says Inter Black uppercase for headlines. The packaging sets product names in serif. Newsreader here, chosen to sit next to Merriweather rather than replace it.
  • Hairlines, not slabs. v2.0 says "structural black borders instead of whitespace". The box uses 1px rules. So does this.
  • Kept exactly: radius 0 everywhere, no shadows, no accent colour, black and white core, JetBrains Mono for data, the AP mark untouched.

Compliance

Zero em dashes. No "diagnose", "treat", "cure". The readout says "That is not a diagnosis". Conflict-free receipt verbatim. No membership sold or priced. No ashwagandha, no EFSA-claim ingredients, no TRT availability. Every number in the panel is sourced from thresholds.md. Not pre-flighted yet: needs /compliance-preflight before build.
