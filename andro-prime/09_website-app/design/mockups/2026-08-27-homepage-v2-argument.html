Clinical context

"In range"
is a statistical
band, not a
health band.

And it is true of
every marker we test,
not just the famous one.

A reference range describes where most men sit. It exists to identify clinical deficiency: the threshold at which you are officially recognised as ill. That is a useful line, and it is not the same line as being well.

It is true of every marker, not one of them. Active B12 is the plainest case: the assay calls anything above 37.5 normal, while the NICE guideline our GP works to treats 25 to 70 as an indeterminate zone. Same number, two verdicts, and a standard report only ever shows you one of them.

Two ranges. Nine markers.
You should see both, on all of them.

So we put both on the same scale for every marker on your panel, on your very first result. Not a verdict, not a diagnosis: the context a bare number does not carry.

Design notes: not part of the page

Mockup of the homepage under site-funnel-model.md v2 (2026-08-27). The visual language is held constant against the live site on purpose: rounded-none, no shadows, Inter/Merriweather/JetBrains, 2px and 4px slab borders. The only variable is the argument.

What changed

  1. The hero argument. Live is "Know your numbers in days. Five minutes at home." (speed, convenience, postage). This leads with the understanding gap, which is app value that lands on a single result. Speed, no-GP, lab accreditation and money-honesty survive as the receipt strip directly beneath.
  2. The device is a PANEL, not one marker (rev 2, after Keith). The first pass put testosterone alone in the hero, which contradicts the whole proposition: a man does not know whether it is his thyroid, iron, vitamin D or testosterone. Thesis section 3 had already called this ("testosterone is the marker least able to demonstrate the app": the brand's lead marker is the one that shows a flat line). Six of nine markers now show, testosterone is one row among them, and the markers carrying the story are the ones that actually move.
  3. The two-range argument now lands where the lab and our bands DISAGREE. Active B12 is the cleanest case in the whole panel: the assay's own cut calls 45 normal, our NICE NG239 band calls 25 to 70 indeterminate. Same number, two verdicts. Ferritin does the same at 30 to 100. That is a far stronger demonstration than a marker where both ranges agree.
  4. The free layer is on the page. "We give the thinking away" plus the demo account. Neither exists on the live site; the demo does not exist at all yet.
  5. Kit CTAs read "Start a baseline" rather than "Order test". The kit is framed as how a record starts, which is the v2 §1a distinction.
  6. The 9-marker panel leads structurally, with Kit 1 and Kit 2 demoted beneath an "already know which question you're asking?" line. The old "start with what's bothering you most" headline assumed he knows, which is the assumption the thesis exists to reject.
  7. "A number is a fact. A record is an answer." Carries the app without selling the membership, and states the cold-start honestly ("one result cannot tell you which direction you are going") rather than promising a trend nobody has yet.
  8. Nothing sells or prices the membership. v2 rule. There is no membership CTA, no £47, and no offer anywhere on this page.

Decisions this mockup makes that are yours, not mine

1. Copy hierarchy rail 2. The conflict-free positioning decision ratified "speed / no-GP hook earns the click, money honesty second." This demotes speed from the H1 to a receipt line. That is a change to a ratified rail and needs your call, not a copy tweak.
2. "Your bloods came back normal." Assumes the reader has already been tested. A good portion of traffic has not been. The quiz and kit paths still work for them, but the H1 speaks past them. Alternative framing available: "You have the number. Nobody explained it."
3. Kit prices shown are the current live ones (£99 / £119 / £179). Gap-analysis decision #5 is still open, and the 2026-08-27 first-month decision now puts a month of membership inside the kit price, so these are placeholders.

Compliance

No "diagnose", "treat" or "cure". The readout says "That is not a diagnosis". No per-customer interpretation and no "GP-built" or "personalised report" framing. No ashwagandha, no TRT availability, no EFSA-claim ingredients. The conflict-free receipt is stated verbatim. Not yet pre-flighted. This needs /compliance-preflight before it goes near a build, and the two-range wording needs Ewa.

Data provenance

Every value, band edge and marker position in the device is sourced from 04_products/results-engine/thresholds.md and commented inline with its arithmetic. Nothing is eyeballed. The first pass of this mockup invented an optimal threshold and put the marker at the wrong percentage, which is how a layout guess becomes an unevidenced health claim, so the rule now is that a mockup either cites the number or renders visibly fake data.

Finding, now RESOLVED and the doc corrected. thresholds.md contradicted itself on Active B12: the summary table recorded <25 / 25-70 / >70, the per-marker section still showed <37.5 / ≥37.5 under "CODE CHANGE RECOMMENDED". The code settles it (classifier.ts:325-327): the three-band scheme ships, ratified by Ewa 2026-06-16 and shipped the same day in 4f05ad6. The stale section was corrected 2026-08-27, and the same sweep miss was found and fixed on ferritin. 37.5 was never a competing band: it is Vitall's assay reference, which we display as the lab's verdict, against our tighter action bands. Both are shown here on purpose, and their disagreement is the product.
Open with Ewa, and used here anyway: thresholds.md flags that the ferritin 30-100 band should be relabelled from "suboptimal" to "borderline / indeterminate", and that there is no high-ferritin GP route at all. This mockup uses "borderline". If Ewa lands elsewhere, the chip changes.

Noted while building: the live homepage labels its dashboard preview "Patient Analysis". "Patient" is clinical framing on a Phase 0 wellness surface; this mockup uses "Your result" instead. Worth a separate look at the live page.
